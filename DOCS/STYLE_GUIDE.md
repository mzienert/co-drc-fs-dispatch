# Style Guide

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

- **Deep Burgundy**: `#5A1A1A`
- **Turquoise**: `#4A9B9B`
- **Rich Gold**: `#D4AF37`

### Neutrals

- **White**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Black**: `#000000`

## Accessibility Guidelines (508 Compliance)

### Color Usage Rules

**Important**: Gold (`#D4AF37`) and Turquoise (`#4A9B9B`) do not meet WCAG AA contrast requirements when used as text on white backgrounds.

**Approved Usage:**
- ✅ Use Gold and Turquoise for large decorative elements
- ✅ Use Gold and Turquoise on dark backgrounds (e.g., Deep Burgundy)
- ✅ Use Gold and Turquoise for borders, icons, and graphic elements
- ✅ Use Deep Burgundy, Black, or White for body text on contrasting backgrounds

**Prohibited Usage:**
- ❌ Do not use Gold or Turquoise for body text on white backgrounds
- ❌ Do not use Gold or Turquoise for small text or critical information on light backgrounds

### Accessible Color Combinations

- White text on Deep Burgundy background
- Black text on White background
- Deep Burgundy text on White background
- Gold text on Deep Burgundy background
- Turquoise text on Deep Burgundy background
